These 42 .pk files are a subset of Andries E. Brouwer's public archive of
Johnson-graph partitions, dated 22 March 2010.

Source: https://aeb.win.tue.nl/codes/partitions/

They are included here only so the 36 constant-weight codes in this
repository can be rebuilt and checked. They are not original to this work.
The filenames are Brouwer's (part<n>.<wmin>_<tag>.pk).
